Motivic homotopy theory

Quand : 10 juin 2026 à 14:00

Où : IHP - Bâtiment Borel

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

Morel and Voevodsky introduced motivic homotopy theory to apply ideas and concepts from algebraic topology to cohomology on algebraic varieties. In this talk I will explain how this theory is build and why such ideas can be useful.
