Framed polytopes and higher categories

Quand : 28 mai 2026 à 11:00

Où : IHES

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

A framed polytope is the convex closure of a finite set of points in ℝⁿ together with an ordered linear basis. An n-category is a category that is enriched in the category of (n-1)-categories. Although these concepts may initially appear to be distant peaks in the mathematical landscape, there exists a trail connecting them, blazed in the 90's by Kapranov and Voevodsky.
