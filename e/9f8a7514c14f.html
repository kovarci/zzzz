A day around optimal transport and functional inequalities

Quand : 17 septembre 2026 à 09:00

Où : 45 rue d'Ulm, 75005 Paris

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

This is a small 1 day meeting around optimal transport and functional inequalities. One of its main goals is to foster exchanges and collaborations between IMJ and Orsay (PARMA Inria team) around these topics. It is of course open to anyone interested in these topics, but the number of participants is unfortunately limited.
