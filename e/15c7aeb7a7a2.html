Introduction to Nori motives

Quand : 10 juin 2026 à 15:30

Où : IHP - Bâtiment Borel

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

Let k be a subfield of the complex number. "Being a Nori motive is the best structure one can put on the singular cohomology of the complex points of an algebraic variety over k." I will try to give a precise meaning to this sentence by defining Nori motives and explain its main properties.
