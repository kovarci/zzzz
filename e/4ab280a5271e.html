No-go theorems for higher-spin charges in AdS2

Quand : 28 mai 2026 à 14:00

Où : Salle des séminaires

Higher-spin conserved currents and charges feature prominently in integrable 2d QFTs in flat space. Motivated by the question of integrable field theories in curved space and by results of the conformal bootstrap, we will consider the consequences of higher-spin currents for QFTs in AdS2.
