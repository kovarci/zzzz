CFT and QFT from the Fuzzy Sphere

Quand : 25 juin 2026 à 14:45

Où : IHES

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

We use the Fuzzy Sphere technology to extract new 3d Ising CFT data and examine its properties in light of expectations from ergodicity. In our analysis, a microscopic construction of the special conformal generators plays an important role. We also study 3d Ising Field Theory, namely, the Ising CFT deformed by a relevant operator.
