CayleyPy-Holography: Mathematical and String Theory Methods for AI

Quand : 16 juillet 2026 à 14:00

Où : IHES

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

We present a new approach to one of the central problems in AI: the construction of embeddings (latent representations) inspired by ideas from holographic string dualities, in particular the AdS/CFT correspondence. Our central conjecture is that, for a broad class of graphs, there exists a dual polygon together with a map ("holography") that sends graph vertices to lattice paths inside the polygon
