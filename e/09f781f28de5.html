CATS 8 - A conference in honour of Gabriele Vezzosi's 60th birthday

Quand : 28 septembre 2026 à 09:00

Où : La Vieille Perrotine

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

The 8 th edition of the "CATS" conferences will take place from September 28 th to October 2 nd , 2026 at "La Vieille Perrotine" , Île d'Oléron, France CATS 8 will be the occasion to celebrate Gabriele Vezzosi's 60 th birthday, and will be part of the ANR project " DAG-Arts: Derived algebraic geometry applied to representation theory and singularity of schemes ". Registration is open until Februar
