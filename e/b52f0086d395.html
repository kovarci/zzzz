STATISTICAL INFERENCE IN LARGE MULTI-WAY NETWORKS

Quand : 15 juin 2026 à 11:00

Où : La doua

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

We propose a new method to estimate structural parameters in multi-way networks while controlling for rich structures of fixed effects. The method is based on a series of classification tasks and is agnostic to both the number and structure of fixed effects.
