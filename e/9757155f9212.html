Quand : 7 juin 2027 à 09:00

Où : LMO

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

This conference will gather international experts working on different aspects of the Langlands program, on the theory of automorphic forms, and on geometric representation theory, to discuss recent progress in these fields.
