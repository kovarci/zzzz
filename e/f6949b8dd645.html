Quand : 8 juin 2026 à 13:30

Où : UFR de mathématique et d'informatique

The goal of this event is to introduce young researchers to the use of machine learning frameworks and automatic differentiation in scientific computing . This one-week program covers the principles of automatic differentiation, JAX , and its modules for scientific computing, along with examples of ML interaction, application-oriented code examples, and hands-on practical sessions.
