Stabilization of a fluid-structure interaction system

Quand : 17 juin 2026 à 11:00

Où : IMT

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

In this talk, we present the analysis of a stabilization problem arising in a fluid-structure interaction system coupling the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with an Euler-Bernoulli equation. In the first part, after introducing the model and the stabilization problem, we discuss some aspects of the regularity of the solutions to the system.
