Boundary contacts for reflected random walks in the quarter plane

Quand : 12 juin 2026 à 10:30

Où : Tours

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

Reflected random walks in the quarter plane arise naturally in probability theory, queueing systems, statistical physics, and combinatorics. In this talk, I will discuss the local time spent on the reflection boundaries.
