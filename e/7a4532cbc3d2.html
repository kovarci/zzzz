Constructing Singular 3-Manifolds from Expanding Thurston Maps

Quand : 12 juin 2026 à 10:30

Où : IHP, 11 rue Pierre et Marie Curie, Paris 5e

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

The class of expanding Thurston maps on S² possesses rich combinatorial structure. Using these maps as a template, we construct a new dynamical system by taking the metric quotient under the identifications induced by the dynamics: a singular 3-manifold with a small set of singularities equipped with a homeomorphism.
