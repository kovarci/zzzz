XMath Physics Day is a one-day workshop taking place on December 7 at Laboratoire Mathématiques d'Orsay (LMO). It will bring together early-career and senior researchers in mathematical physics working on resurgence, topological string theory, and scattering amplitudes. Organisateurs : Veronica Fantini (veronica.fantini@universite-paris-saclay.fr), Claudia Rella (rella@ihes.fr)
