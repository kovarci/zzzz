Calculus of Variations, Optimization and Image Processing - on the occasion of Antonin Chambolle's 60th birthday

Quand : 21 janvier 2027 à 09:00

Où : INRIA Paris

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

This conference aims to bring together researchers from optimization, calculus of variations and image processing to celebrate Antonin Chambolle's contributions to these fields. The event brings together collaborators, colleagues, friends, and former students to celebrate his scientific achievements and the thriving community around them.
