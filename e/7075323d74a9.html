Energy exchange models, random walks and the KPZ class

Quand : 28 mai 2026 à 14:00

Où : ICJ

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

Kipnis, Marchioro, and Presutti (KMP) introduced in 1980 a very simple Markovian model for energy exchange in a chain of harmonic oscillators. In many ways, the KMP model behaves like the symmetric simple exclusion process (SSEP).
