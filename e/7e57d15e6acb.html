Darij Grinberg : Shuffles in the symmetric group algebra

Quand : 4 juin 2026 à 14:00

Où : IHP - Bâtiment Borel

Organisé par : Institut Henri Poincaré

Ever since the famous 1992 work of Bayer and Diaconis, it has been known that random shuffles of a deck of cards (with the back side up) can be modelled as elements of the group algebra $\mathbb{R}[S_n]$ of the symmetric group $S_n$.
